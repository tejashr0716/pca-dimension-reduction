THE METHOD

A small pipeline. Clear decisions.

01

Validate & clean

Check the CSV and component count. Select numeric features, drop incomplete rows, remove constant columns, and report what changed.

02

Put features on equal footing

Subtract each feature’s mean and divide by its standard deviation so units alone do not dominate the result.

03

Find principal directions

Full SVD finds orthogonal directions ordered by explained variance. Project rows into the selected component space.

04

Measure what remains

Inspect explained and cumulative variance, then download the scores. Fewer components means less data—and potentially lost signal.

About the 99.7% benchmark. The sample deliberately has 12 latent factors across 100 correlated features. Its measured result is favorable to PCA; arbitrary CSVs may retain much less variance with 12 components.
